📊 Company Valuation Model – Excel

📌 Project Overview

The Company Valuation Model is an advanced financial analysis and business valuation project developed using Microsoft Excel.

This project focuses on evaluating a company's financial performance, forecasting future growth, calculating intrinsic value, and estimating the fair value of its shares using professional financial valuation techniques.

The model combines historical financial analysis, Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation, Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC), Comparable Company Analysis, Sensitivity Analysis, and an interactive Valuation Dashboard.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze historical financial performance.
- Forecast future revenue and profitability.
- Calculate Free Cash Flow (FCF).
- Estimate the Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC).
- Perform Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation.
- Calculate Enterprise Value and Equity Value.
- Compare company valuation with market valuation.
- Evaluate different valuation scenarios.
- Present key financial insights through dashboards and charts.

📈 Financial Charts – 5 Visualizations

The project includes five financial charts to visualize company performance and valuation results.

1. Historical Revenue vs Forecast Revenue

- Compares historical revenue with projected revenue.
- Highlights future business growth.

2. Net Profit Trend

- Tracks changes in net profit over time.
- Helps evaluate profitability.

3. Free Cash Flow Trend

- Visualizes projected Free Cash Flow.
- Helps assess cash generation capacity.

4. Enterprise Value vs Equity Value

- Compares Enterprise Value and Equity Value.
- Highlights the difference between business value and shareholder value.

5. Valuation Sensitivity Analysis

- Evaluates valuation under different WACC and growth assumptions.
- Helps understand the impact of changing financial assumptions.
